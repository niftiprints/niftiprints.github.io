Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 31, 2026

1. Introduction

Nifti Decoded ("we", "our", "the Service") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our content publishing service.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following information:

  • Account Authorization Data: When you connect your TikTok or YouTube account, we receive an access token that allows us to publish content on your behalf. We do not store your password.
  • Basic Profile Information: Your username and account ID from connected platforms, solely to identify where content should be published.

3. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect personal messages or private content
  • We do not collect browsing history or location data
  • We do not collect payment or financial information
  • We do not sell any user data to third parties

4. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to:

  • Publish educational video content to your authorized social media accounts
  • Maintain your connection to the Service
  • Check the status of published content

5. Data Storage and Security

Authorization tokens are stored securely on encrypted servers. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. Access tokens are only used for their intended purpose (content publishing) and can be revoked at any time.

6. Third-Party Services

Nifti Decoded integrates with the following third-party services:

  • TikTok: For video publishing via the Content Posting API
  • YouTube: For video publishing via the YouTube Data API v3
  • Pexels: For sourcing royalty-free stock images used in videos

Each third-party service has its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review them.

7. Data Retention

We retain your authorization data only as long as you actively use the Service. If you revoke access or request deletion, we will remove your data within 30 days.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Revoke access to your social media accounts at any time
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Request information about what data we hold

9. Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

11. Contact

For privacy-related questions or data deletion requests, contact us at niftiprintsshop@gmail.com.
